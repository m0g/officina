description: >-
  Officina Kreuzberg opened in 2016 as a shared workshop and has grown into a
  coworking space run as a collective. There's no company behind it: the members
  rent the space together, decide together and share the work of running it.
  That keeps the rent low and the place ours.<br/><br/>We're a mixed group –
  developers, designers, illustrators, architects, researchers, a policy
  coordinator, a copywriter, a filmmaker. What most of us have in common is who
  we work for: NGOs, non-profits, social and environmental projects.
