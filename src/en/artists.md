From 2019 to 2021 Officina Kreuzberg hosted an artist-in-residence programme. Six artists each spent two to three months working in the space and our darkroom, supported by the members, and showed the result in a final exhibition here. The programme is on pause; we're planning to bring it back in 2027 – follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/officinakreuzberg/) to hear about the next open call.
