Officina Kreuzberg is a shared workspace near Hermannplatz, on the Kreuzberg–Neukölln border. There's no operator and no front desk: the space is run by its members, a collective of freelancers and remote workers in software, design, research, architecture, policy and communications – many of us working with NGOs and social projects.

What you get: a desk with 24/7 access, fast internet, a printer, a big kitchen and a terrace where we have lunch or an after-work drink together. Hasenheide park is across the street, supermarkets and lunch places around the corner. What we ask: that you take part in the life of the space.

Currently available: fix desks, flex desks and, from November, one private room for up to three people.
